Chess UNO Rulebook

♟️ What is Chess UNO?

Chess UNO seamlessly marries traditional FIDE chess with the tactical dynamism of UNO cards. Chess remains the primary strategic battlefield—cards provide tactical maneuvers, piece revivals, and board control without breaking the sacred laws of chess.

Standard Chess Rules Always Apply:
• Check & Checkmate
• Stalemate & Draws (Threefold repetition, 50-move rule)
• Kingside & Queenside Castling (O-O, O-O-O)
• En Passant Captures & Pawn Promotion
🏆 Winning the Match

The ultimate victory condition is identical to classical chess: Checkmate the opponent's King. Cards provide tactical momentum, but sound chess fundamentals will always decide the champion.

⚡ The Energy Economy

Energy is your personal tactical reserve, accumulated by converting Number cards in your hand. Each player stores up to 20 Energy.

⚡ Draw Card (Cost: 6 Energy)

Spend 6 Energy on your turn to draw 1 extra card directly from the deck into your hand (subject to the 5-card hand maximum).

♻️ Recycle Card (Cost: 3 Energy)

Spend 3 Energy, select 1 unwanted card from your hand to discard, and instantly draw a fresh replacement card from the deck.

Pro Strategy: Early in the opening, play high Number cards (7, 8, 9) to build an Energy reserve. Later in the game, spend energy to dig for Game-Winning special cards like Wild Draw Four or Skip!
⚰️ The Graveyard Pool & Revival Rules

Whenever a piece is captured on the board (by standard capture or En Passant), it is transferred to that player's Graveyard.

Piece Revival Rules:
• Draw Two (+2): Revives 1 friendly Pawn to its original file column (Rank 2 for White, Rank 7 for Black), or nearest empty square.
• Wild Draw Four (★+4): Opens a selection modal to pick any captured friendly Queen, Rook, Bishop, Knight, or Pawn, and place it on an empty square of your choice.
• Safety Check: Placing a revived piece cannot leave your King in check.
• Pawns: Cannot be revived onto Rank 1 or Rank 8.
• Kings: Can never be captured, destroyed, or revived.
❓ Frequently Asked Questions
❓ Can I ignore a Check on my King by playing a card?
No. Standard FIDE check rules are absolute. When your King is attacked, you must legally escape check on your turn. Skip cards cannot freeze an enemy piece if that piece is your opponent's only way out of check.
❓ Can I revive a King with Wild Draw Four?
No. Kings are permanent commanders and never enter the Graveyard. Only Pawns, Knights, Bishops, Rooks, and Queens can be revived.
❓ Can cards create illegal board positions?
No. Every card effect is validated by the chess engine before execution. If a Reverse swap or piece revival would leave your King in check, it is prevented and the card remains safely in your hand.
❓ How many cards can I play per turn?
You can play at most 1 card per turn. Playing a card is always optional.
❓ What happens when the card deck runs out?
The discard pile is automatically shuffled back into a fresh 108-card draw deck with zero interruption to play.
❓ Does Undo and Redo work with cards?
Yes! The undo/redo engine saves complete immutable snapshots of the chessboard, card hands, energy levels, and graveyard. Undoing a move restores card state perfectly.
